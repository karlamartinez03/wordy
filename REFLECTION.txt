After looking through Paul's solutions I was happy to see that his implementation and mine are functionally very similar.
Outside of that, the main differences are stylistic, such as in the compile method in BinaryExpressionNode. I chose to use an if/else statement while the sample solution uses a return that skips past that else block.
One thing this comparison highlighted is how much the interpreter and compiler implementations mirror each other structurally, despite doing fundamentally different things.
It was interesting to see first hand what makes some languages faster within the operating system and how these are built.
